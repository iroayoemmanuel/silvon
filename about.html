About SILVON

Born from a love of the table

SILVON began as a small supper club — a group of friends cooking for strangers in a candle-lit loft. The idea was simple: treat every guest like family, and every ingredient like treasure.

Today, that philosophy lives on in our Omuaran dining room. We work directly with local growers and fishermen, letting the season write the menu and our chefs interpret it with craft and restraint.

We believe fine dining should feel intimate, not intimidating — a place to slow down, share, and savor.
